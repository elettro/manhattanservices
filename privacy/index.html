Privacy policy

How information is handled when you visit ManhattanServices.com or contact its publisher.

Publisher: Elettro IncorporatedEffective August 19, 2026

What information does the site collect directly?

The public directory does not require a visitor account. Information you choose to send through a linked contact channel is used to review and respond to the request. Hosting, font, video, and linked third-party services may process technical information under their own policies.

Site access and technical data

Web hosting and delivery systems may process an IP address, browser information, device information, requested URLs, timestamps, referrers, and security logs to deliver the site, maintain reliability, and prevent abuse. The publisher does not operate a public user-account system on this site.

External services and links

The site uses externally hosted fonts and includes YouTube-related content. Provider, agency, and Elettro links lead to third-party websites. Those services may process data, set cookies, or apply their own privacy terms after a visitor loads or opens their content. Review the destination’s policy before submitting personal information.

Contact and listing requests

When you send a correction, listing, partnership, or marketing request through a linked Elettro contact channel, the information may include your name, business information, contact details, message, and supporting documents. It is used to evaluate and respond to the request and maintain relevant records.

Choices and questions

You control whether to follow external links or submit contact information. Browser settings and extensions offer controls for cookies and third-party resources. Privacy questions or requests related to information sent to the publisher should identify ManhattanServices.com and the relevant communication.
