Trust and Accuracy

Editorial standards

The rules used to research, write, review, update, and correct ManhattanServices.com.

Published by Elettro IncorporatedUpdated August 19, 2026

What is the editorial goal?

Every page should give Manhattan visitors a clear next step, identify who owns the information, separate editorial listings from commercial promotion, and support important claims with reliable sources.

Source priority

  1. Official government and agency resources for public procedures.
  2. First-party provider pages for current service descriptions and contact paths.
  3. Recognized professional organizations for safety or category guidance.
  4. Original explanations written specifically for Manhattan visitors.

Provider listings

Listings are based on category relevance, service-area fit, accessible information, and a clear customer contact path. A listing is not a certification, ranking guarantee, quality guarantee, or endorsement. Visitors should independently verify pricing, availability, insurance, licenses, references, and terms.

AI-assisted production

Automation and AI tools may support research organization, drafting, formatting, structured data, and quality checks. Human editorial responsibility remains with Elettro Incorporated. Pages must be reviewed for accuracy, usefulness, internal consistency, unsupported claims, and source quality before publication.

Updates and corrections

Pages display a reviewed or updated date when materially checked. Broken official links, changed procedures, incorrect provider information, and factual errors receive priority. Correction requests should identify the exact page, disputed statement, and reliable supporting source.
