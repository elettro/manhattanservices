Fast answers about using this directory.

Does ManhattanServices.com provide the listed services?

No. ManhattanServices.com is an independent directory. Contact each provider directly for availability, pricing, insurance, terms, and booking.

How are providers selected?

Listings are reviewed for category relevance, Manhattan or New York City availability, accessible service information, and a clear contact or booking path. Inclusion is not a guarantee or endorsement.

Is ManhattanServices.com part of New York City government?

No. The site is privately published by Elettro Incorporated. City-help pages link to official NYC agencies so residents can reach the proper government resource.
