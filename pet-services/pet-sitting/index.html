Manhattan Pet-Care Guide

Pet sitting services in Manhattan.

Plan dependable drop-in, vacation, cat, medication, and overnight care inside your Manhattan home.

Published by Elettro IncorporatedReviewed August 19, 2026

How do you choose a pet sitter in Manhattan?

Schedule a meet-and-greet and review experience, references, insurance, background screening, access security, backup coverage, and emergency procedures. Create written instructions for every visit, including timing, feeding, medication, litter or walks, home tasks, updates, and veterinary contacts.

Choose the right care format

Drop-in care works for pets that need scheduled feeding, water, litter, walks, medication, and companionship. Overnight sitting adds a longer presence in the home. Define exact arrival and departure expectations because “overnight” does not automatically mean continuous daytime care.

For puppies, senior pets, medical needs, or pets with separation concerns, ask whether the proposed schedule safely matches the animal’s routine.

Create complete written instructions

  • Feeding amounts, times, storage, and restricted foods.
  • Medication names, doses, timing, and administration method.
  • Walking, litter, play, behavior, and household routines.
  • Keys, access codes, alarms, doors, windows, and doorman procedures.
  • Veterinary information, emergency contacts, authorization, and transportation.
  • Photo, message, and visit-confirmation expectations.

Protect your pet and home access

Ask who may enter, how keys are labeled and stored, whether subcontractors are used, and what happens if the assigned sitter becomes unavailable. Tell building staff who is authorized and remove access when the assignment ends.

Before leaving, test locks, carriers, harnesses, medication supplies, food quantities, cleaning supplies, and communication. Leave a backup contact with authority to make decisions if you cannot be reached.

Set a useful visit-report standard

Agree on when updates arrive and what each report should cover. Useful reports confirm arrival, completed tasks, appetite, water, elimination, medication, activity, behavior, and anything unusual in the home. Photos help, but a photo alone does not confirm the full routine. Define when the sitter should call immediately instead of waiting for the normal written update.

Pet-sitting FAQ

What should written instructions include?

Feeding, water, medications, litter or walking, behavior, access, household tasks, veterinary information, emergency contacts, and updates.

Drop-in or overnight care?

Drop-ins cover defined visits. Overnight care adds a longer home presence, but the exact hours and daytime coverage should be written down.

How should medication be handled?

Confirm experience, provide written dosage and timing, demonstrate the routine, and create a missed-dose or emergency plan.
