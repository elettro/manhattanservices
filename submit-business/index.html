Request editorial review for a relevant apartment, pet, building, or neighborhood service.

What should you submit?

Provide the legal business name, website, direct contact path, Manhattan service area, primary categories, concise service description, and any licensing or insurance information relevant to the work. Include the name and role of the authorized representative making the request.

Send Listing Request Through Elettro

Required submission details

  • Legal business name and public-facing brand name.
  • Website and direct customer contact or booking URL.
  • Specific Manhattan neighborhoods or ZIP codes served.
  • Primary and secondary service categories.
  • Relevant licensing, insurance, certification, or permit information.
  • Authorized representative’s name, role, and business contact information.
  • Requested corrections if an existing listing already appears.

Editorial review factors

The directory reviews service relevance, Manhattan availability, accessible customer information, clarity of claims, category fit, and whether the destination offers a useful contact path. Submission does not guarantee acceptance, ranking, placement, or continued publication.

Listing FAQ

Is submission the same as approval?

No. Submission starts an editorial review and does not guarantee publication.

Does a standard listing cost money?

This page describes editorial listing review. Any future paid option should be separately explained and clearly labeled.

Are paid placements labeled?

Yes. Any paid placement, sponsorship, advertising, or lead-generation relationship should receive a clear label near the placement.

Categories currently under review

The directory focuses on services with clear Manhattan relevance. Current categories include apartment cleaning, moving, furniture assembly, mounting, handyman work, dog walking, pet sitting, cat care, building services, and related neighborhood support. Businesses should request the narrowest accurate category instead of submitting a long list of loosely connected services.

A strong listing destination gives visitors enough information to evaluate fit before making contact. Keep the public website current with service descriptions, service area, business identity, contact information, and any material restrictions.

What improves a review request

Use factual language and link to supporting pages. Explain what the business does in Manhattan, who performs the work, and how customers start. Identify information that has changed since any existing listing was created. Requests with complete, verifiable details require less follow-up and move through editorial review faster.
