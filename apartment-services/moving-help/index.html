Manhattan Apartment Guide

Moving help for Manhattan apartments.

Plan estimates, packing, elevators, insurance, loading access, and building coordination before moving day.

Published by Elettro IncorporatedReviewed August 19, 2026

How should you plan a Manhattan apartment move?

Start with both buildings. Confirm move hours, elevator reservations, service entrances, insurance requirements, deposits, and loading access. Then request written estimates using the same inventory, stairs, distance, packing needs, and timing so each quote covers the same work.

Get a complete written estimate

Describe every large item, box estimate, disassembly need, stair flight, elevator condition, long hallway, storage stop, and difficult carry. State whether you need packing, unpacking, materials, disposal, or temporary storage.

  • Ask whether pricing is hourly or fixed.
  • Confirm minimum hours, travel time, truck, tolls, parking, and materials.
  • Ask what changes the final price.
  • Review valuation or damage-coverage options before signing.

Coordinate both buildings

Manhattan moves often depend on management approval. Ask each building about permitted hours, freight-elevator windows, service entrances, floor protection, move deposits, certificates of insurance, loading zones, and who meets the crew.

Give the mover every requirement early. A missing document or elevator reservation can delay the move before the first box leaves.

Moving-day checklist

  1. Photograph valuable furniture and existing property conditions.
  2. Label boxes by room and priority.
  3. Keep identification, medications, keys, chargers, and critical documents with you.
  4. Protect elevators, hallways, and building surfaces as required.
  5. Confirm the destination contact and access window.
  6. Review the final inventory and paperwork before the crew leaves.

Choose the right level of moving help

A labor-only crew may fit a small move when you already have transportation, materials, and building approval. A full-service mover may handle packing, truck, loading, transportation, unloading, and selected setup. Decide which responsibilities stay with you before comparing prices. The cheapest headline rate is not the cheapest final move when truck, travel, materials, stairs, or waiting time sit outside the quote.

Manhattan moving FAQ

What should a moving estimate include?

Labor, truck, travel, materials, stairs, elevators, long carries, parking, minimum hours, coverage, taxes, and potential extras.

Why do buildings request a certificate of insurance?

Management uses it to verify the mover’s required insurance before granting access.

What should I reserve first?

Service entrances, elevator windows, loading access, and any required building move appointment.
