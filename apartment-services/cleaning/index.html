Manhattan Apartment Guide

Apartment cleaning services in Manhattan.

A practical hiring guide for recurring cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in work, move-out work, and one-time apartment resets.

Published by Elettro IncorporatedReviewed August 19, 2026

How should you hire an apartment cleaner in Manhattan?

Define the rooms, tasks, frequency, supplies, access instructions, pets, and add-ons before requesting quotes. Compare the same written scope from each provider. Verify insurance, building requirements, arrival windows, cancellation rules, and how quality concerns are handled.

Choose the right cleaning type

Recurring cleaning usually covers routine floors, surfaces, bathrooms, kitchens, dusting, and trash. Deep cleaning adds detail work such as baseboards, buildup, fixtures, and harder-to-reach areas. Move-in and move-out cleaning should define empty cabinets, appliances, closets, and post-move debris.

  • List every room and priority task.
  • Identify add-ons such as inside ovens, refrigerators, windows, or cabinets.
  • State whether the apartment is occupied, empty, furnished, or under renovation.
  • Confirm who supplies products, tools, bags, and a vacuum.

Manhattan building details matter

Tell the cleaner about the doorman, service entrance, freight elevator, stairs, parking limits, intercom, keys, pets, and any certificate-of-insurance requirement. A low quote loses value when access delays shorten the appointment or create extra fees.

For occupied apartments, secure valuables and provide clear instructions for fragile surfaces, specialty stone, wood, artwork, and household products that should not be used.

Questions to ask before booking

  1. Is the price hourly, flat-rate, or based on the apartment size?
  2. Which tasks and supplies are included?
  3. Is the company or cleaner insured?
  4. Will the same person return for recurring visits?
  5. How are keys, entry codes, and cancellations handled?
  6. What happens if a task is missed or damage occurs?

Plan the first visit carefully

Use the first appointment to confirm realistic timing and priorities. Walk through the apartment, identify surfaces that need special care, and separate essential tasks from optional add-ons. For recurring service, agree on a stable checklist and a process for changing priorities. A short written recap after the first visit gives both sides the same reference for future appointments.

Apartment-cleaning FAQ

What should an apartment-cleaning quote include?

Rooms, tasks, supplies, add-ons, duration, total price, arrival window, cancellation terms, and exclusions.

Should I ask whether the cleaner is insured?

Yes. Ask about liability coverage and whether your building requires a certificate of insurance.

What makes Manhattan cleaning different?

Building access, elevators, compact spaces, parking, service entrances, pets, and strict arrival windows often affect the job.
