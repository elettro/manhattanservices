Manhattan apartment services FAQ

Practical questions to resolve before hiring.

What should I ask an apartment service provider before booking?

Confirm the exact scope, total price, supplies, insurance, arrival window, cancellation terms, building access needs, and who handles cleanup or disposal.

Do Manhattan buildings require a certificate of insurance?

Many staffed buildings require vendors and movers to provide a certificate of insurance. Ask management before booking so the provider has time to submit the required document.

How should I compare apartment-service quotes?

Give every provider the same job description, photos, access details, timing, and material requirements. Compare the complete written scope instead of the headline rate alone.
