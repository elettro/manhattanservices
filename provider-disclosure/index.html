Provider disclosure

What a listing means, how providers are selected, and what visitors still need to verify.

Published by Elettro IncorporatedUpdated August 19, 2026

Does a listing equal an endorsement?

No. A listing indicates that the provider or resource appears relevant to a category and offers an accessible information or contact path. It does not guarantee quality, availability, pricing, licensing, insurance, safety, results, or suitability for a specific job.

Selection factors

The directory reviews category fit, Manhattan or New York City service relevance, clarity of public information, and whether visitors have a direct path to learn more or make contact. Official agencies receive priority for government procedures.

Visitor verification

  • Confirm current service area and availability.
  • Request written pricing, scope, and terms.
  • Verify insurance, references, licenses, and permits when relevant.
  • Review building, pet-safety, cancellation, privacy, and dispute policies.
  • Confirm the identity of the person or company performing the work.

Commercial relationships

The marketing-help section promotes services from Elettro Incorporated, the publisher of this directory. If a provider pays for placement, sponsorship, advertising, or lead generation in the future, the relationship should receive a clear label near the placement. Standard editorial listings are not presented as paid recommendations.

Corrections and removal

Businesses may request corrections to names, links, service descriptions, or service areas. Removal and correction requests should come from an authorized representative and include reliable supporting information. The publisher retains editorial control over category structure and inclusion.
