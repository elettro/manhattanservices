About ManhattanServices.com

A clearer starting point for apartment help, pet care, official NYC resources, and local business support.

Published by Elettro IncorporatedUpdated August 19, 2026

Who publishes this site?

ManhattanServices.com is an independent directory published by Elettro Incorporated. It is not a provider marketplace, booking agent, government agency, emergency service, or representative of the businesses and public agencies linked from its pages.

What the directory covers

The site organizes practical Manhattan needs into clear categories. Apartment pages cover cleaning, moving, handyman work, mounting, assembly, and related services. Pet pages cover walking, sitting, visits, cat care, and recurring support. City-help pages direct residents to official NYC reporting and agency resources. Marketing pages connect local businesses with Elettro.

How the information is maintained

The editorial team reviews page relevance, provider and agency links, visible claims, structured data, internal links, and page dates. Official resources receive priority for government procedures. Provider listings are informational and require independent verification by the visitor.

Corrections are reviewed against reliable first-party or official sources. Material updates receive a new reviewed date.

Ownership and commercial relationships

Elettro Incorporated owns and publishes ManhattanServices.com. The marketing-help section promotes Elettro services. Provider listings elsewhere do not imply ownership, partnership, certification, or endorsement unless a relationship is clearly disclosed on the page.
