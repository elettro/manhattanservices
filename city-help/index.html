Common city and building issue categories

Start with the issue type that best matches what is happening.

No Heat or Hot Water

Lack of heat or hot water is one of the most urgent apartment issues for Manhattan residents. This category helps users understand the next step and where to report the problem.

Official NYC resources
  • NYC 311: No Heat / Hot Water
  • File a NYC 311 complaint
  • NYC HPD Tenant Rights Info

Apartment Maintenance Problems

When serious apartment issues go unresolved, renters need a clearer path for documenting and reporting the problem.

Official NYC resources
  • HPD Online (Building Complaints)
  • NYC Housing Maintenance Code
  • NYC 311 Service Requests

Noise Complaints

Noise issues are common in dense city living. This category helps users identify whether the issue is building-related, neighbor-related, or city-related.

Official NYC resources
  • NYC 311: Noise Complaints
  • NYC Noise Code Information
  • Report noise via 311

Trash and Recycling Issues

Missed pickup, improper trash handling, and cleanliness issues often need a reporting route rather than a hired service.

Official NYC resources
  • NYC Department of Sanitation
  • 311: Missed Collection / Trash Issues
  • Submit sanitation request via 311

Street and Parking Problems

Blocked access, parking issues, and other local street-related concerns often require city channels, not private vendors.

Official NYC resources
  • NYC 311: Illegal Parking and Blocking Driveways
  • NYC Department of Transportation
  • Report street issues via 311
