Official NYC Resource Guide

No heat or hot water in a Manhattan apartment.

Notify the owner, document the condition, file an official NYC 311 complaint, and keep every record.

Independent directory, not an NYC agencyOfficial links reviewed August 19, 2026

What should you do first?

Report the condition to the property owner, manager, or superintendent. Save the time, method, and response. If service is not restored, file a complaint through the official NYC 311 heat or hot-water page and keep the complaint number.

Call 911 for fire, gas odor, serious medical distress, or another immediate threat to life or safety.

Step-by-step complaint path

  1. Notify the owner or manager. Report the problem clearly and request restoration.
  2. Document the condition. Keep dates, times, photos, videos, temperature readings when relevant, and all messages.
  3. File with NYC 311. Use the official complaint page or call 311 within New York City.
  4. Save the complaint number. Keep it with your timeline and follow-up notes.
  5. Track the issue. Record inspections, repairs, temporary changes, and continued outages.
  6. Use official HPD resources. Review NYC HPD tenant information if the condition continues.

Build a useful record

A complete timeline helps you explain the problem consistently to management, 311, inspectors, or an appropriate housing resource. Record the address and affected areas, start time, indoor conditions, communications, complaint numbers, inspection activity, and repair claims.

Do not rely on memory. Keep screenshots and written notes together. If neighbors are affected, each household should follow the official guidance that applies to its situation.

Follow up with precise information

When you follow up, use the same complaint number and state whether the condition remains unchanged, returned after a temporary repair, or affects additional rooms. Add new dates, readings, photos, and management responses to the existing timeline. Clear, consistent records help the official agency understand the history without forcing you to reconstruct events during every contact.

Heat and hot-water FAQ

Where do I report the problem?

After notifying the owner or manager, use the official NYC 311 heat or hot-water complaint service.

What records should I keep?

Dates, times, readings when relevant, photos, videos, messages, responses, complaint numbers, and inspection information.

Is this an NYC government page?

No. ManhattanServices.com is an independent directory linking residents to official NYC resources.
